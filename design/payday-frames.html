Why Home changes: what hides the next payout today

On the current Home (11 panels, Nov 2026), Maria cannot see her next payout:

  • No panel shows it. None of the 11 panels shows the payout amount or its arrival date.
  • Balance (position 9, BalanceSummaryCard) shows only the total USD balance, with no pending/available split and no payout.
  • It sits below the fold. On a 1440×900 screen she has to scroll to panel 9 before she can start looking.
  • It takes 4 clicks: "View balances" > Payouts tab > "Upcoming" filter > open the row. Amount and arrival date only appear together after click 4.
  • Pending only: Balance shows $0.00, with no hint of the pending amount or when it becomes available, so the money looks gone.
  • After a refund: the balance turns red with a minus sign, with no reason, no sign the payout was canceled ("Canceled" is only in the Payouts list), and no word on when payouts restart.
  • The top of Home goes to other things: Setup guide banner, Today chart and Recommendations.
